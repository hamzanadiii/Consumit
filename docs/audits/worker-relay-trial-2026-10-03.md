## Implemented behavior

New playback starts with the configured direct provider waterfall. Direct discovery and recovery finish that bounded tier before entering the relay-approved waterfall. Relay failures stay within relay; direct and relay have independent health/cache namespaces and exclusion histories. Each tier permits twenty seconds of discovery, with forty-five-second browser requests, fifty-second gateway timeouts and a sixty-second recovery lock. With relay enabled, the rolling ten-minute recovery budget covers the eligible direct plus relay pool, capped at 128; direct-only configuration keeps three switches. Expiry renewal retains the active delivery path, including after a relay capability expires, while the edge itself always rejects expired capabilities. Source identity changes on direct → relay so late errors cannot burn the replacement. Existing Watch attachment logic retains position/play intent and immediately attaches a changed delivery path. Optional separate audio remains direct and source replacement retains its existing cancellation behavior. Until the owner adds a supported embed provider, relay exhaustion returns unavailable. P2P is a later measured supplement for popular matching streams.

API and Worker independently gate each provider against exact approved media hosts. Encrypted capabilities bind URLs, origin, expiry, header hints and nesting. Playlist references and redirects are checked before fetching, credential/caller headers never travel upstream, and cookie/other upstream response headers are stripped. Manifests are bounded and rewritten; native media and HLS segments stream with range support, cancellation and a stalled-read deadline. Variables/content steering, DASH relay, byte transforms and multi-range requests fail closed or remain unavailable. Default invocation URL logs are disabled; sampled custom records contain aggregate operational fields only.

Advancing local playback wall time is counted separately for direct and relay with conservative pause/seek/stall/background treatment. Delivery accompanies queued browser events. Cloudflare account metrics supply billable requests/CPU; sampled custom request/byte/duration logs supplement them without claiming CPU or accurate billing totals.
