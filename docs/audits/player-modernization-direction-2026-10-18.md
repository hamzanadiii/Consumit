# Player modernization direction contract

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Keep the film or episode visually dominant. Consumit's controls should be easy to find and understand without turning the viewing surface into a dashboard.

**OWN-WORLD:** Preserve the midnight cinematic field, supplied artwork and title logos, and signal orange for active choices. Use locally hosted Inter for interface text and retain the existing cinematic title face. Use the established 3px micro, 5px control and 6px panel geometry. Panels distinguish themselves through charcoal tone and fine borders rather than extra-round corners.

**STORY:** Members can tell whether a choice is a Consumit server or an external player, compare readable names, search the current group, and see what the selected source cannot do. Preparation, buffering and failure name the current state. Recovery offers a useful next action and keeps the native checkpoint and subtitle choice.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Retain the Watch composition and 16:9 media stage. Keep the title and source toolbar above the stage, with transport and timeline close to its lower edge. Sources opens a fixed Consumit Server / External players choice above a scrolling ordered list. On phones, play, seek, mute, captions, and fullscreen stay in the first control row; Sources and Settings sit in a compact, safe-area-aware second row.

**FORM:** Operate. The selected form is the user's pinned extension of Consumit's existing cinematic player, at assigned direction index 3; concept seed `4618bac3` was printed by the concept-seed command. The user-approved plan pins the existing layout and visual identity, which takes precedence over any conflicting catalog form. Signature interaction: a fixed source-family choice scopes search and favorites while retaining Admin ordering. Motion: short opacity and transform transitions, no layout resizing, and no authored movement under reduced motion.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
