# Provider embeds after Direct and Relay

## 2026-10-15: personal ordering and advertising opt-out

ADR 0005's personal-order amendment permits an explicitly ranked Embed before Direct or Relay; the original three tiers remain the default. Current provider, identity, region and source validation remains mandatory. Advertising Off rejects sources declaring `frameSandbox: 'none'` during resolution, including personal-order and manual choices, so automatic recovery offers compatible servers instead of requiring unsandboxed popup permissions. On retains the existing provider-scoped exception and frequency-limited browsing integration; display banners remain removed. Every external player is labeled with its unavailable Consumit subtitles, progress and auto-next capabilities. A resolved frame is still not decoded-playback evidence. Owned attempt continuation supersedes the historical one-request three-tier timeout below; each source request is bounded and the finite cycle is cancelable. Local implementation and verification limits are recorded in [the release audit](../audits/reliable-playback-2026-10-15.md).

Startup recovery also accepts an optional configured `providerId` on `/v1/playback/resolve`. An explicit choice resolves only that eligible provider through its saved delivery tier; it never silently starts a different server. Disabled, audio-only, wrong-region, wrong-kind, unsupported anime audio and independently unapproved Relay rows remain ineligible. Auto keeps the Direct → Relay → Embed waterfall. The configured provider snapshot can be requested before a session exists, without upstream probing. Canonical identity, host validation, quotas, circuit state and member progress remain independent of this choice.

The owner requested five provider frames on 2026-10-04. Watch now tries configured Direct providers, then independently approved Relay providers, then Embed providers, with manual Server selection across those tiers. This supersedes the provider-embed deferral in ADRs 0003 and 0005; public Consumit `/embed` and `/v1/embed` routes remain deferred.

An Embed is a distinct validated HTTPS `iframe` source, never a media URL or Worker capability. Movie/series frames use exact TMDB season/episode identity. Anime frames use exact AniList identity and require the requested SUB/DUB lane to exist; a dual-audio frame may require choosing that language inside its player. Provider-specific behavior stays behind adapters. Configured host, expiry, credential and kind validation applies to fresh and cached results, and saved delivery settings cannot turn a media adapter into a frame adapter.

The 2026-10-12 recovery extends canonical frame construction to all configured movie/series frame adapters: a server-side page probe must not gate a published exact-host iframe URL, because provider browser verification can reject the lookup caller while allowing the member's frame. Anime adapters still require their reviewed exact-identity and requested-audio mapping; only the subsequent frame-page probe is removed. Returning a candidate does not claim playback success or bypass provider verification. The existing sandbox policies are unchanged.

Consumit retains its native player and controls for Direct and Relay. Watch composes an external frame only for Embed, keeping the title, reload, server selection, retry/fallback and episode navigation outside it. Frames allow scripts, same-origin functionality, forms and presentation; popup and top-navigation permissions are withheld. A provider may refuse this sandbox or require browser verification; those failures remain visible and the member can choose another server or explicitly open the validated player in a separate tab using a `noopener noreferrer` link.

On 2026-10-08 the owner asked for in-frame playback from the providers that refuse any sandbox. MegaPlay and VsEmbed react to the `sandbox` attribute itself: a permissive sandbox that also allows pop-ups and top navigation is still refused, while the same validated URL loads and plays as a plain iframe. Their frame adapter therefore declares `frameSandbox: 'none'`, the validated source carries that policy to Watch and Admin, and ProviderFrame renders only that source without a sandbox attribute; every other frame keeps the standard sandbox and its withheld popup/top-navigation permissions. This is a deliberate, provider-scoped weakening of the boundary for exactly those two host-pinned frame URLs: such a frame can open pop-ups and navigate the top window, so it never applies to media or relay sources, the Worker grant path, member progress, or session identity. We do not alter provider scripts, and a provider that fails verification keeps its visible failure, outside recovery controls and isolated new-tab link rather than being disguised as success.

Without a reviewed playback-event protocol, iframe load is document evidence only. Frames do not report decoded playback, seek, viewing time, progress, completion, automatic next episode, cast or separate audio. They neither overwrite a native checkpoint nor claim to resume it. Admin previews share this boundary and distinguish page resolution from browser playback evidence. Each resolution tier has a twenty-second deadline; the browser allows sixty-five seconds and the playback socket/gateway seventy.

On 2026-10-11 the owner requested the measured production provider routes be deployed. Vixsrc Frame adds the exact `vixsrcframe` Embed registration on `vixsrc.to` for canonical TMDB movie and episode paths. It constructs the provider's published player URL without an OVH document probe, because the measured server lookup is challenged while the browser player decodes. Issuing a frame remains candidate resolution, never playback-health evidence. The same reviewed plain-frame exception applies to this provider: its standard sandbox control did not play, and its movie and series plain-frame controls decoded. Its provider controls, possible popups/top navigation and unavailable progress sync remain the existing external-player boundary. The original Vixsrc Direct registration remains separate and unchanged.

The requested additions are ReAnime Frame and MegaPlay Frame for anime; APIPlayer Frame, EmbedAPI Frame and VsEmbed Frame for movies/series; standalone Rigel and Rive Citadel as Direct; Vidnest, StigStream and VaPlayer as Relay. Relay approval uses finite, independently configured exact media hosts. The previous rotating-host recipes are not extended to arbitrary provider results. The Worker remains on the existing Free deployment. Live evidence and sample limitations are recorded in the provider-addition audit.
