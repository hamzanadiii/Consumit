# Whole-app frontend experience coverage

## Objective and authority

Improve the full Consumit frontend, with experience quality taking priority over decorative changes. This is a coverage and evidence ledger, not a replacement product or design specification. Product behavior remains in `docs/PRODUCT.md`, visual authority in `DESIGN.md`, shared interfaces in `docs/COMPONENTS.md`, and platform constraints in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

The goal remains active. Passing a few journeys or the unit suite does not prove the entire app is complete. Preserve the approved Consumit Player while improving its task flows.

## Requirements for each surface

- The member job has a clear entry, action, result, and next destination.
- Guest, member, owner, visitor, and collaborator behavior is checked where applicable. Sign-in retains the actual destination.
- Loading, empty, populated, unavailable, invalid, failed-save, and conflict states have appropriate recovery where the surface supports them.
- Text, artwork, controls, focus, and reading order work on narrow phones, larger screens, short landscape, and both themes.
- Keyboard operation and visible focus match the control's semantics. Navigation, browser history, and reload preserve expected state.
- Stored progress, private drafts, audience choices, and member records are preserved by the flow.
- Verification follows the behavior through the public interface; rendered captures are inspected, and existing shared components/tokens remain the visual reference.
