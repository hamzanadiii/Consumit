## Product definition

If automatic startup cannot create a playback session, Watch offers Choose another server in its existing error panel and retains the below-player server strip. Selecting a configured server starts that exact source with the same title or episode and saved native position. A failed choice remains selected for Try again; Auto resumes automatic selection using the saved personal order or the default delivery order. External frames retain their existing progress limitations.

Consumit is a cinematic streaming and personal taste-discovery product for movies, series, and anime. Its promise is: **watch immediately and remember what you love.**

The active product combines three jobs:

1. Help a member confidently choose what to watch.
2. Start playback with as little friction as possible.
3. Preserve history, progress, favorites, ratings, reviews, and lists.

## Product principles

Optional analytics measures grouped public page visits and excludes profiles, private libraries, authentication and Watch, and stays separate from the first-party anonymous performance measurements. By default it asks guests and members on public browsing surfaces whether to allow Google Analytics cookies, with Both Allow analytics and Decline analytics available at equal emphasis; declining preserves normal browsing and playback. A deployment may instead set `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_PROMPT=off`, which hides the prompt entirely and starts analytics without a stored prompt choice; Privacy Policy section 7 remains available so anyone can decline later and stop collection on that device.

Account recovery preserves entered details on request failure. Account creation never labels an edited username using an older availability response, and repeated verification-code requests cannot overlap verification. Unexpected page failures offer retry and Home; a broken root layout offers reload. Subtitle discovery that stops early reports a recoverable failure instead of continuing to check indefinitely.

- **Taste before volume.** Explain why a recommendation fits instead of presenting an undifferentiated poster wall.
- **Playback before promotion.** The path from a title to a playing source is the primary product path.
- **Progress belongs to the member.** History and resume position remain stable when the playback source changes.
- **Provider failure is normal.** Recovery should feel like source switching, not a technical error dump.
- **Profile identity is public; taste sections are controlled separately.** Favorites, Ratings, Reviews, Lists, Network, Likes, Tags, and Watchlist are visible by default and can each be hidden in Settings. Watched titles, Diary, History, active playback, resume progress, and account data stay private. Consumit does not collect a profile location.
- **Lists are a way to share taste.** Members can create ordered lists from their profile or a title page, search movies, series, and anime, add notes, and choose Public or Private. New lists start public. A list is public only while both the list and its owner's Lists section are public. Visitors can share a public list by link or save the original to their private Saved Lists; saved references follow the curator's edits and become unavailable if hidden or deleted.
- **Public taste does not opt members into viewing trends.** Anonymous viewing trends have a separate off-by-default setting. The Home row uses only title-level results from opted-in progress in the last seven days and appears when at least five members contribute to a title. It reveals no identities, counts, or live status.
- **Editorial, human language.** Product copy is concise, cinematic, warm, and taste-aware.

## Core experience loops

### Discover and watch

`Home/Search/Profile/List -> Title -> Watch -> Progress -> Resume`

The member sees a confident recommendation, opens its detail, and starts a valid source. If that source fails, Consumit offers or performs a fallback without losing progress.

### Remember and express taste

`Watch -> History/Diary -> Rating/Review -> Favorites/Lists -> Profile`

Watching is retained independently of the provider. Explicit choices improve the member's profile and future discovery.

A member's portrait stays consistent across the navigation header, profile, archives, Settings, People search and reviews. A saved portrait takes precedence; otherwise the current public username determines the same generated portrait everywhere. Visiting another member keeps the signed-in member in the header and the visited member in the profile.

### Publish and discover taste

`Settings → Profile sections → People search → Profile → Follow`

Guests browse profiles, whose identity is public by default. Settings exposes a control for each public taste section; owner-only Watched, Diary, History, playback, progress, and account data remain private. Public tags aggregate Review tags only, and only while Reviews is public. A visitor can read a list only when the list itself and its owner's Lists section are public. Visitor tabs are Overview, Favorites, Ratings, Reviews, Lists and Network; Network groups Followers and Following behind one tab with an internal view choice. Owners retain access to hidden sections. Profile and list pages remain `noindex`.

People searches usernames and display names case-insensitively, including substrings, with a three-character minimum and a 100-character maximum. Exact usernames rank first. The global selector retains its 200 ms debounce and keyboard behavior; People-only reads bypass catalog services.

Following requires sign-in and never happens automatically after sign-in. Hiding Network suppresses its public counts and direct visitor collection reads while preserving relationships; the owner keeps access. Username aliases redirect to canonical profile links; Share copies that link.

Circle/activity feeds, messaging, follow requests, follow notifications, collaborative list editing and global public-list search remain outside this slice.

For your next watch is private to signed-in members and derives up to twenty recent unique movie, series and anime seeds from actual account Watch History and owned completed device progress, including the 95 percent threshold. It combines catalog recommendations from the eight freshest resolved seeds with recency-decayed reciprocal-rank fusion, cross-seed agreement, genre continuity and a light genre/format diversity pass. These catalog-derived recommendations carry no Taste Match or individualized-confidence claim.

Computed Taste Match and personalized ranking return only when their signals and explanation are implemented. Live routes never fill sections with invented member evidence.

## Movies and Series browsing

Movies (`/movies`) and Series (`/series`) help a member choose a title through a rail followed by a filterable catalog. Movies labels its rail “Now playing” (current releases); Series uses “Trending now” (weekly trending). Opening artwork leads to the title detail.

The main catalog starts with Popular and offers genre, year, Trending, and Top rated controls. Home's movie and series View all links open the matching Trending or Top rated feed, with its sort selected and results heading aligned. Direct links retain that feed on reload; unsupported or repeated feed values open Popular. Available genre choices come from the initial current and popular titles; they are not an exhaustive genre directory. A chosen filter queries the catalog beyond the titles already shown while retaining the selected feed, and the rail remains independent. Reset returns to the default Popular catalog.

Scrolling near the end appends another page without duplicate titles. A keyboard-accessible Load more action remains available. Filter changes start a fresh result set; loading announces progress and failed requests offer Try again while retaining previously loaded titles. An empty result offers Clear filters; the final page explains that the member has reached the end.
